Daybook Helper

Visible video and background music, tracked only on this Mac.

  1. Connect the local bridge in Daybook → Settings → Install Daybook Helper.
  2. In Daybook → Settings, copy your pairing code if it was not prepared automatically.
  3. Paste it below, save, and reload your video tab once.

The helper reads media progress, sound state and geometry, including embedded frames. It exchanges these signals and the top-level domain with Daybook over encrypted local messages. Visible video counts as watching; audible background YouTube and common music sites count as Music. No activity uploads, page text, titles, screenshots or audio recording. Website access is needed for embedded players; private-window access is off.
