Daybook Video

Count real video playback, even while you work in another app.

  1. Install the local bridge using the included setup script.
  2. In Daybook → Settings, copy your video pairing code.
  3. Paste it below, save, and reload your video tab once.

The extension inspects video play/pause/progress, including embedded frames. It sends only the top-level website domain, playback state and timing through a local native bridge. It does not send data to an internet service, record titles/page text, or enable private-window tracking. Muted video still counts. Permission on all websites is needed for embedded players hosted on another domain.
