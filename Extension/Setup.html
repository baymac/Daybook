Count video while you work

Brave, Chrome and Edge can report real video progress to Daybook. This works in background tabs and embedded frames. A playing video is counted even without keyboard or mouse input.

  1. In Daybook Settings, click Show extension folder. Keep that folder in its current location.
  2. For Brave, double-click install-bridge.command in that folder. This installs a local native-messaging manifest. It does not modify browser security settings. For Chrome or Edge, run python3 install_bridge.py --browser chrome or --browser edge from the folder.
  3. Open brave://extensions (or chrome://extensions / edge://extensions). Turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, and select the VideoExtension folder containing manifest.json. Review the browser's website-access permissions.
  4. In Daybook Settings, click Copy pairing code. Open the Daybook Video extension's options (click its toolbar icon), paste the code, and save with sending enabled.
  5. Reload the stream tab once. Keep Daybook running with tracking on and Count video playback enabled. Its Settings status will show the detected domain.

What the permission means: detecting embedded players needs extension access to all websites, because players may be hosted on a different domain. The implementation only inspects video playback properties, and sends the top-level domain, play/pause status and timing to a local helper. It does not send data to an internet service or save/send titles, text, screenshots, full page URLs or audio samples. Private-window access is not enabled.

How time is counted: a two-hour video plus two hours of work happening at the same time counts as two hours of computer usage. Video playback and foreground time are also shown separately. Per-app/site and category activity totals may overlap. Pauses, lost signals, sleeping and stopped tracking do not create an unlimited playback session.

Detection begins after setup and a tab reload. Old missing playback cannot be reconstructed. Browser throttling or unusual players can delay signals; undetected gaps are not guessed. Only HTML video in regular HTTP(S) pages is supported, not native player apps, browser-internal pages or audio-only elements. Use a stable app location and re-run the bridge installer if you move Daybook. Closing Daybook stops saving activity.
